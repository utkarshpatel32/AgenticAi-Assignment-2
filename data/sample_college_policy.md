# Fictional assignment demonstration policy

This document is fictional coursework content and is not official AIDTM policy.

## Payment portal failure
If the payment portal fails, preserve the transaction reference and a screenshot, then contact the institute to confirm payment status. Do not assume a deadline extension or a next-working-day grace period unless the institute confirms it.

## Fee record correction
A student may submit a request to correct a fee record. The request remains pending until an authorised reviewer approves it. A rejected request must not change the record.

## Programme change
A requested programme change is a sensitive academic-record change. It must be reviewed by an authorised human approver before the student's programme field is updated.
